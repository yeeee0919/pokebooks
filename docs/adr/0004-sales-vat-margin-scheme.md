# Commercial sales VAT uses individual margeregeling

Used Pokémon cards are typically bought without a VAT invoice (private sellers, Cardmarket, inbreng). Output VAT on a commercial SELL is therefore the individual margin scheme — 21/121 of max(0, selling price − commercial COGS) — not 21% of full omzet. The owner can turn VAT off per sale or override the amount. Global (pooled) margeregeling was rejected because mixed platforms and per-sale control matter more than period pooling.
