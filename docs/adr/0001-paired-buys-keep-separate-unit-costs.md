# Paired commercial buys keep separate unit costs

When a commercial BUY creates a biz+priv scope pair, quantity/date/product stay linked, but each side keeps its own `pricePerUnitEUR`. Commercial cost is the tax book value (often fair market value on inbreng); private cost is the owner's actual acquisition cost. Syncing one cost into both ledgers would contaminate KOR/IB inventory with personal cost basis (or the reverse).
